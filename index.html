🧬 Nutrient Quick Guide

💪 Protein

Helps build and maintain muscles and other tissues.

Sources: dal, beans, soy, paneer, eggs, dairy, fish, chicken.

🩸 Iron

Important for making haemoglobin and supporting normal body function.

Sources: lentils, beans, leafy greens, seeds, eggs, meat and fish.

🦴 Calcium

Important for bones and teeth.

Sources: dairy, tofu, ragi, sesame and fortified foods.

🌾 Fibre

Supports normal digestion and helps make meals more filling.

Sources: whole grains, vegetables, fruits, beans and seeds.

☀️ Vitamin D

Helps the body use calcium and supports normal bone health.

Sources: fortified foods and some animal foods.

🧬 Vitamin B12

Important for blood cells and normal nervous-system function.

Sources: dairy, eggs, fortified foods, fish and meat.

🍊 Vitamin C

Supports normal immune function and helps absorb plant-based iron.

Sources: citrus fruits, guava, peppers, tomatoes and vegetables.

🥕 Vitamin A

Supports normal vision and immune function.

Sources: carrots, pumpkin, leafy greens and dairy.

⚙️ Zinc

Supports normal growth, development and immune function.

Sources: seeds, legumes, dairy, eggs, fish and meat.

🥑 Healthy Fats

Provide energy and help the body absorb certain vitamins.

Sources: nuts, seeds, avocado and suitable plant oils.

🍚 Carbohydrates

Provide energy for everyday activity.

Sources: rice, roti, oats, potatoes, fruits and grains.
