🥗 Nutri Facts

General educational nutrition information. Individual nutritional needs vary, so this is not medical advice.

Life stage Focus nutrients Useful food groups Simple reminder
👦 Growing children & teens Protein, iron, calcium, zinc, vitamin D, fibre Dal/beans, dairy or alternatives, vegetables, fruits, grains, nuts/seeds Regular balanced meals matter during growth.
👧 Growing girls Protein, iron, calcium, vitamin D Beans, lentils, leafy vegetables, dairy/fortified alternatives, fruits Iron-rich foods are particularly useful to discuss with a healthcare professional when needed.
🧑 Adults Protein, fibre, vitamins, minerals, healthy fats Vegetables, fruits, whole grains, legumes, nuts/seeds Build variety into the weekly diet.
👴 Older adults Protein, calcium, vitamin D, fibre, hydration Protein-rich foods, vegetables, fruits, whole grains, calcium-rich foods Hydration and adequate protein are important.

💪 Protein

Helps build and maintain muscles and other tissues. Vegetarian sources include dals, beans, lentils, soy, dairy and nuts.

🩸 Iron

Important for normal blood function. Legumes, leafy vegetables, beans, fortified foods and seeds can contribute.

🦴 Calcium

Important for bones and teeth. Dairy and calcium-fortified alternatives are useful sources.

⚙️ Zinc

Supports normal growth and immune function. Beans, lentils, dairy, nuts and seeds can contribute zinc.

🍊 Vitamin C

Fruits and vegetables such as citrus fruits, guava, tomatoes and peppers can provide vitamin C.

🌾 Fibre

Whole grains, vegetables, fruits, beans and lentils are useful sources of dietary fibre.
