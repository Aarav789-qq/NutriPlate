🌦️ Seasonal Suggestions

Hydrating foods, fruits, vegetables and lighter meals.

Warm meals and hygienically prepared foods can be comforting.

Ideas: vegetable soups, khichdi, roasted snacks, dal-rice.

Warm balanced meals with vegetables, legumes and grains.

Ideas: soups, stuffed rotis, dal, vegetable rice bowls.
