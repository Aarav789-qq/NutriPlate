🥗 Nutri Facts

General nutrition information for different stages of life.

👦 Growing Teens

Focus on regular balanced meals containing protein, whole grains, fruits, vegetables, calcium-rich foods, iron and zinc.

👧 Growing Girls

A varied diet can provide protein, iron, calcium, vitamin D and other nutrients needed during growth.

🧑 Adults

Aim for a balanced plate containing vegetables, fruits, grains, protein foods and healthy fats.

👴 Older Adults

Adequate protein, calcium, vitamin D, fibre and hydration become especially important with age.
